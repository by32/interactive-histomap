Who is who

    Each figure stands for 5 soldiers. Greek numbers follow Herodotus 7.202–203; the Persian host is drawn at 30,000 and its true size is disputed.

    About this reconstruction

    The terrain is a schematic model built from the ancient descriptions, not survey data. The coastline follows the geological reconstruction of Kraft, Rapp, Szemler, Tziavos & Kase (Journal of Field Archaeology 14, 1987), which places the 480 BC shore within tens of metres of the cliffs; today's plain is river silt. The exact course of the Anopaea path is still debated. Narration follows Herodotus 7.175–239 with Diodorus 11.4–11 and Plutarch where noted.
